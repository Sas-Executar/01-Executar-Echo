EXECUTAR — PNG BRAND PACKAGE v1.0

IDENTITY
Primary palette:
#000000 Black
#2D2D2D Dark Gray
#9A9A9A Gray
#E5E5E5 Light Gray
#F8F8F8 Off White
#FFFFFF White

Typography direction:
Monospaced, uppercase, expanded tracking.
The PNG wordmark supplied in this package is rasterized from a monospaced system font.
No font file is distributed.

FOLDERS
01_logo_mark   Transparent logo marks in primary, black and reverse variants.
02_wordmark    EXECUTAR wordmark in black and white PNG.
03_lockups     Horizontal and stacked brand lockups.
04_app_icons   Opaque PNG exports for iOS, Android and PWA.
05_favicon     PNG favicon sizes.
06_social      Social avatars and Open Graph brand tiles.
07_brand_preview Preview sheet.

USAGE
- Use PRIMARY assets on white/light neutral backgrounds.
- Use DARK/REVERSE assets on black or very dark backgrounds.
- iOS App Store icons are opaque by design.
- PWA maskable exports use a reduced mark scale for the safe zone.
- Keep generous clear space around the symbol and wordmark.
