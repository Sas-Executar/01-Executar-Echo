# Research Protocol

## Contents
- Research question

- Search loop
- Freshness
- Contradiction handling
- Stop criteria

## Research question
Convert the request into a bounded question with object, period, geography, unit of analysis and decision relevance.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer, in order when applicable: primary official sources and first-party records; standards/regulators and peer-reviewed evidence; reputable institutional or industry research; high-quality secondary reporting; community material only for experience/sentiment and clearly labeled.

## Search loop
1. Inspect user-provided evidence first.
2. List claims requiring external verification.
3. Search each major claim class separately rather than relying on one broad query.
4. Open the underlying source when available; do not rely on snippets alone.
5. Record date, scope, population and source type.
6. Seek an independent source for high-impact or contested claims when practical.
7. Update the framework shortlist if evidence reveals a better analytical lens.

## Freshness
Use current sources for prices, regulations, market conditions, product capabilities, company data and other volatile facts. Stable methodological definitions can rely on bundled framework metadata.

## Contradiction handling
Record contradictory claims with source, date, scope and likely reason for divergence. Do not silently average or choose one source.

## Stop criteria
Stop searching when the selected framework fields are sufficiently supported, key conflicts are understood, marginal searches repeat known evidence, and remaining gaps are explicitly documented.
